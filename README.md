# Unicorn_game
Game Title

Unicorn Clicker

Main Goal

Collect 1,000 unicorns by clicking on a unicorn. The goal is to reach 1,000 unicorns and win the game.

Player Controls

Mouse click or tap to collect unicorns.

Click the Rainbow Power upgrade button to purchase the upgrade.

Gameplay

The player starts with 0 unicorns.

Each click on the unicorn earns 1 unicorn.

Players can save their unicorns to purchase Rainbow Power.

Once purchased, Rainbow Power permanently doubles the number of unicorns earned per click.

Scoring

Each normal click earns 1 unicorn.

Each click after purchasing Rainbow Power earns 2 unicorns.

The game displays the player's current unicorn total.

Win/Lose Rules

Win: Collect 1,000 unicorns.

Lose: There is no losing condition. Players can keep trying until they reach the goal.

First Version

The first version will include:

A cute pastel pink and purple background.

A large clickable unicorn.

A unicorn counter.

A Rainbow Power one-time upgrade.

A progress indicator toward 1,000 unicorns.

A winning screen when the player reaches 1,000 unicorns.
